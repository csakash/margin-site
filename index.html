The quiet power of writing in the margins

Every serious reader eventually discovers that the best ideas show up mid-paragraph — not before you start, not after you finish.

The note you take in the moment is worth ten you promise to write later. Most note tools ask you to leave. A margin never does.

It sits right next to the text, patient and empty, costing nothing to keep open all day.

What it does

Everything you need to think while you read. Nothing else.

One hotkey, one calm panel, plain files on your own disk.

Floats over full-screen apps

A floating panel that shows on every Space, including full-screen Chrome, without stealing focus from the page until you click in.

Minimises to a line

⌥ Space folds it into a thin glowing line on its edge. No text, no logo, no distraction.

Markdown, styled live

Headings, lists, checkboxes, quotes, code and dividers as you type. Press / for every block.

Drop anything in

Images, videos, files and links. Drag a sentence from a page and it becomes a quote with its source.

Snap & clip

Grab a region screenshot, or clip the page you're on from Chrome, Arc, Safari, Brave or Edge.

All notes, one pull away

A drawer slides up from the bottom and pushes your note up, never over it. Search everything instantly and filter by to-dos, media, links or quotes.

Placement

Any edge. Any corner.

Drag it and the screen dims, eight spots light up and a beam shows where it will land. Flick it and it carries on to the far side.

Lightweight & private

Built to stay open all day.
